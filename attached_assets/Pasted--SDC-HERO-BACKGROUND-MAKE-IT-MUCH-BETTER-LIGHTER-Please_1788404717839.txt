## SDC HERO BACKGROUND: MAKE IT MUCH BETTER + LIGHTER

Please improve the homepage hero background again.

The current hero background has become **too dark**. I want it to be significantly **lighter, cleaner, brighter and more premium**.

### DESIGN DIRECTION

Keep the SDC blue identity, but move away from the dark/navy-heavy appearance.

The hero should feel:

**Bright + Clean + Premium + Calm + Fresh + Modern + Medical**

Think of a high-end modern dental clinic, not a dark-tech website.

### COLOR BALANCE

Use:

* Bright/soft blue
* Light blue
* White
* Very subtle blue gradients
* Soft blue-tinted surfaces

Deep navy can still be used VERY selectively for contrast, but it should NOT dominate the hero background.

The overall hero should feel visually light.

### BACKGROUND

Create a beautiful minimal background using subtle depth:

* Soft blue-to-light-blue tonal transition
* Very subtle abstract dental/medical-inspired shapes
* Gentle light areas
* Soft gradients
* Clean negative space
* Extremely subtle texture if useful

The background should have enough visual interest to feel premium, but remain simple.

### IMPORTANT

A background image is NOT necessary.

If there is no genuinely excellent dental image, keep the hero image-free and create a beautiful abstract blue background.

Do NOT use a random stock image just to fill space.

### AVOID

Absolutely avoid:

* Very dark backgrounds
* Almost-black navy
* Heavy dark gradients
* Neon blue
* Cyberpunk effects
* Huge blobs
* Excessive glassmorphism
* Excessive particles
* Busy patterns
* Strong glowing effects
* Distracting animated backgrounds

### HERO READABILITY

The text must remain extremely clear against the background:

**Top Rated Dental Clinic in Mumbai**

**Dr Somil Dental Clinic**
**Your Perfect Smile Starts Here**

The CTA buttons should have strong contrast and remain immediately visible.

### SUBTLE ANIMATION

Keep the background mostly static.

If adding animation, use only extremely subtle movement such as:

* Very slow gradient movement
* Gentle light shift
* Minimal floating shape movement

Nothing distracting.

### FINAL VISUAL TARGET

The hero should feel like:

**a bright, premium, modern dental clinic website with a sophisticated blue identity.**

It should be visually impressive because of **color balance, spacing, typography, composition and subtle depth**, not because of heavy effects.

Most importantly:

**LIGHTER. CLEANER. BRIGHTER. LESS DARK. MORE PREMIUM.**

Do not change the SDC information or appointment functionality. This update is specifically focused on making the hero background much more beautiful and visually balanced.
